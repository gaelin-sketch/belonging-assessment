09 — Components

Numbered lists

Two shapes. Insights are findings, stacked and colour-rotated. Steps are actions, on a timeline, each split into what you own and where help comes in.

1

Staff value belonging highly; the organisation delivers it unevenly.

Staff rate belonging 8.16 of 10 as essential to their own thriving. But the overall Belonging Index of 49 sits in the At-risk band, just below the threshold.

2

Immediate teams are a real strength; the connections between teams are not.

Within immediate teams, belonging reaches 62.5%. On the extended team it falls to 28.8%.

3

Exclusion is rare, but ambiguity is widespread — and ambiguity is not neutral.

Outright exclusion stays well under 8% at every level. The challenge is diffuse: uncertainty about where one stands.

1

Build in capacity that cultivates connection

Targets the structural belonging gap (62.5% immediate → 28.8% extended)

The county owns

Belonging never crossed team boundaries because nothing in how the county works was built to carry it there. Treat connection as infrastructure.

Where support helps

A partner maps where connective capacity is actually missing across divisions, then designs structures that fit each unit's real workflow.

2

Equip managers to prioritise the three indicators

Targets ambiguity, the dominant state, through the people best positioned to convert it

The county owns

Ambiguity is resolved in the daily experience of being managed, not by programmes. Make managers fluent in all three indicators.

Where support helps

A partner gives managers a working understanding of each indicator and a shared language to read where their team sits.
