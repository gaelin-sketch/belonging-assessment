Gaelin Elmore's Individual Belonging Assessment

Do you BELONG at work?

In a world where belonging matters more than ever, it's becoming increasingly rare to belong at work. One of the biggest obstacles, beyond direct opposition to belonging, is a simple reality: belonging is hard to measure.

Most surveys ask whether you feel like you belong. Both you and I know it's more complicated than that. Fitting in can feel like belonging if you've never experienced what it's like to truly belong at work. Brené Brown says "the opposite of belonging is fitting in." This assessment measures what belonging is actually made of: the authenticity you bring, how your environment receives it, and three research-grounded conditions: psychological safety, social connection, and co-creation. And to make sure it's as accurate as possible, we apply these elements across the different levels of your organization: your immediate team, your direct leader, and your extended team members.

ImmediateThe team you work with every day, where belonging is felt first and lost fastest.
LeadershipYour supervisor and the leaders above them, the relationship that shapes what feels possible.
ExtendedThe wider organization beyond your team: other divisions, other buildings, the whole.
